Changing a catalog row does not by itself rewrite an old packing line. Changing the quantity of that old packing does. That is `BA-P08-001`.

## Materials

`createMaterial` / `updateMaterial` (`src/server/materials.ts`) require a positive section with at most 2 decimal places. Uniqueness is name plus both section numbers. `40.125` was rejected. Sort order `7` was stored.

A batch copied section `40×20` at insert. The material was then renamed and set to `50×25`. The batch stayed `40×20`. Purchase volume uses the batch section (`reports.ts:129`), not the catalog section. Archive of a material that already has a batch succeeded. Delete was refused with the archive hint. Concurrent creates were not run.

## Details

Detail number is not unique. Two details were both number `1`. That matches the comment in `nomenclature.ts:160–164`.

`updateDetail` writes the new length and prisadka flags and does not read `BlankStock` or `DetailStock`. This run did not leave an operation line on the detail after the length change, because the packing edit had already replaced that line. A flag change on a detail that already holds `DetailStock` was not measured.

## Product BOM

`validateProduct` rejects a repeated detail id and a foreign material. It does not reject an empty composition, zero, or a negative quantity.

An empty composition was stored on the product that was later deleted. A line `quantity: -1` was stored (`BA-P08-005`). `snapshotUpakovkaApply` skips `quantity <= 0`, so a pack does not consume that line. `buildCostProductRows` adds the same quantity into the article (`cost-report.ts:452`), so the live cost formula would subtract. That ruble was not computed in this run.

Active SKU uniqueness is per column. A second product with `skuWb` equal to the first product's `skuOzon` (`P08-OZ`) was accepted. That is the condition of `BA-P06-003`, not a new card.

## What an old pack keeps

Sequence on the disposable database, gate off:

1. Product composition was detail A, length 1 m. One pack had consumed it. Blank 1 m stood at `4`. Product stock stood at `1`. The operation line pointed at A.
2. `updateProduct` replaced the composition with detail B, length 2 m. The operation line was still A. The catalog change alone did not move stock.
3. `editProductionOperationQuantity` changed the pack from `1` to `2`.

After the edit the operation line pointed at B, quantity `2`, length `2`. Blank 1 m was restored to `5`. Blank 2 m fell from `5` to `3`. Product stock became `2`. The old physical consumption was replaced by the new composition. `BA-P08-001`.

`ARCH-P1-001` is the other branch: when the gate is on, the same edit throws before the write. The gate stayed off here, which is the live setting. That card was not re-run and is not fixed.

Cancel of an old pack was not called. `reverseUpakovkaOperation` returns the stored lines, not the current composition. That path is unread in this phase.

## Delete and sales

`deleteProduct` counts goals, `ProductCost`, and `ProductStock` only (`nomenclature.ts:553–559`). `Sale.productId` is not a foreign key. A product with a sale and no stock was deleted. The sale row remained, with `productId` still set to the removed id. `BA-P08-006`. Supply was not inserted. `BA-P05-002` (an archived product hidden while its stock remains) was not re-run.
