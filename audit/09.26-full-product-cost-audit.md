## What each expected figure means

These were fixed before the report was read.

- Wood is the money paid for the batch. Sort prices only split it. With no waste and every blank inside this one product, the product carries `1600`.
- Labor is the piece rate once per physical piece. Торцовка follows the blank sort. Присадка follows the operation that was done. Pack is once per product.
- Fastener, box, and insert are the catalog unit price times the BOM quantity.
- Overhead is a confirmed expense on an overhead article in this month, shared by direct cost. An unconfirmed expense is not overhead.
