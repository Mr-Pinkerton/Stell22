## Where the pictures disagree

Disposable instants, business zone UTC+3:

- `2026-10-31T21:30:00Z` is 00:30 on 1 November. Cut of 2 m producing 1 m. Pack of `7`. Cash income `111`.
- `2026-11-15T09:00:00Z` is noon. Hours `2 × 50`. Cash income `500`. Sale revenue `1000`.
- `2026-11-30T21:30:00Z` is 00:30 on 1 December. Pack of `4`. A second cut.

November waste report (business bounds): taken `2`, waste `1`, percent `50`. The December cut is outside.  
November dashboard production: `4`. The pack of `7` is dated 31 October by `workDate.toISOString().slice(0, 10)`. The pack of `4` is dated 30 November, so it falls in the November tile.  
November dashboard income: `500`. The `111` is dated 31 October the same way.  
Sales report for the business month: `1000`. The chart titled «Выручка vs прошлый месяц» sums to `500`.  
Waste chart: first point `31.10` at `50%`, then thirteen zeros through `13.11`. Days `14.11`–`30.11` are not drawn (`i < 14`).
