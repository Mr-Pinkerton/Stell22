## What the money does

`operationEarning` prices an operation from its rate snapshot: hours × hourly, blanks × the sort rate, присадка × the flag that the line actually has, pack × the pack rate. Each amount is rounded to kopecks with `Math.round`. `getSalaryReport` sums those amounts for every unpaid operation. The week and the month limit `Payment.paidAt` only. `markEmployeePaid` locks that set, writes one `Payment` and one `PaymentBatchItem` per operation (`operationId` is unique), then tries `maybeFreezeBatch` for each торцовка batch. Freeze needs `closedAt` and no unpaid торцовка. It does not read присадка, упаковка, or hours.

The card has one «Присадка» field. `createEmployee` writes it into both присадка columns (`employees.ts:21–22`). A later `updateEmployee` does not rewrite snapshots. Archived staff stay on the unpaid report. Deleting a staff member who has operations is refused.

`Payment` is the payroll fact. This action does not insert `CashFlow`. That is the current split, not a missing posting.

Banknotes use `roundCashTo100` (50 and above rounds up to the next 100 ₽). That figure is not written to `Payment`.

## Accrual against the cost report

`loadCostContext` builds `actualProductionRates` from operations whose `workDate` is inside the cost period. Hours on a day that also has piece-work are spread across that employee's units. A day with only hours is paid and is not spread (`payroll.ts:301–304`). The product and blank rows then use that period average, not the snapshot of the operation on the row.

Measured on this database for November 2026:

- Period payroll `737.01`, of which `200` is an hours-only employee.
- A blank cut at snapshot `20` shows `workCost` `31.39`, the rounded period blend.

That is `COST-002` and `COST-004`, already open. It is not a second copy of the same wage and it is not a lost payment. The wage is in `Payment` / the unpaid total. The cost row is an average rate for the period.

`0.5 × 10.01` is `5.01` in both Decimal half-up and the unpaid total.

A line never carried both присадка flags. One operation with both kinds stored `(true, false)` and `(false, true)`. The `else if` in `actualProductionRates` (`payroll.ts:353`) stayed unreached.

## Payment

| Case | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Cut `20` plus one hour `100` | one payment `120`, two items |
| Second call | refused, still one payment |
| Two overlapping calls | one payment |
| Hours inserted while the first row is locked | payment stays `40`; the new row stays unpaid |
| Pay and cancel together | this run paid the row and did not also cancel it |
| Transaction expired while the batch row was locked | `isPaid` stayed false and no `Payment` was left |
| Rename after payment | the history row shows the new name; the amount stays `120` |
| Zero piece rate | payment `0`; cash-flow count unchanged |

An open batch does not freeze when its cut is paid. A finished batch with two cuts freezes only after both are paid, and then has one FINAL row. A later hours payment does not add a second FINAL.
