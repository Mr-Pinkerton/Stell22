## 3. What the policy does

With `custom_branch_policies = true` and exactly one `type: branch` policy named `main`:

- a job that declares `environment: production` is admitted only from `refs/heads/main`;
- other branches are excluded;
- tags are excluded, including a tag named `main`, because no tag policy exists;
- wildcard refs are excluded, because the policy name is the literal `main`.

`can_admins_bypass = false` disables administrator bypass of Environment protection rules at deployment time. Deployment branch policies are enforced for every actor. Required reviewers and a wait timer are absent. They are not required for this ref-isolation objective, and this closeout does not add them.
